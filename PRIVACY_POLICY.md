# Privacy Policy

**LegalSahay**

**Last Updated: April 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to LegalSahay ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the LegalSahay mobile application (the "App").

By using LegalSahay, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information

When you register for and use LegalSahay, we may collect the following personal information:

- **Phone Number**: Used for account creation, authentication, and communication.
- **Name**: Used to personalize your experience within the App.
- **Location Information**: Your state and district of residence, used to provide jurisdiction-specific legal information and connect you with relevant legal resources.
- **Language Preference**: Your preferred language for interacting with the App.

### 2.2 Usage Information

- **Legal Issue Type**: The category of legal issue you are seeking information about, used to tailor AI responses and legal resource recommendations.
- **Chat Messages**: The content of your conversations with the AI assistant, used to provide personalized legal guidance and improve the quality of our AI responses.
- **App Usage Data**: General usage patterns, session duration, and feature interactions, used to improve the App experience.

### 2.3 Device Information

- **Device Identifiers**: Basic device information necessary for push notifications and app functionality.
- **Operating System and Version**: Used for compatibility and troubleshooting purposes.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **To Provide Legal Guidance**: Your information is used to deliver personalized, jurisdiction-aware legal information through our AI-powered assistant.
- **To Improve AI Responses**: Chat interactions help us refine and improve the accuracy and relevance of our AI-generated legal information. All data used for improvement is anonymized and aggregated.
- **To Personalize Your Experience**: Your location, language preference, and legal issue type enable us to customize content to your specific needs.
- **To Authenticate Users**: Phone numbers are used for secure account verification and login.
- **To Communicate with You**: We may send you important updates about the App, changes to our policies, or service-related notifications.
- **To Comply with Legal Obligations**: We may process your data as required by applicable laws and regulations.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Encryption

All personal data is encrypted both in transit (using TLS/SSL) and at rest (using AES-256 encryption). We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

### 4.2 Secure Servers

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by MongoDB Atlas, which maintains SOC 2 Type II certification and complies with international security standards. Servers are hosted in geographically appropriate regions to ensure data residency compliance.

### 4.3 Access Controls

Access to personal data is strictly limited to authorized personnel who require it to operate, develop, or improve the App. All access is logged and monitored.

## 5. Third-Party Services

LegalSahay integrates with the following third-party services to deliver its functionality:

### 5.1 Anthropic

We use Anthropic's AI models to process your legal queries and generate responses. Chat messages are sent to Anthropic's API for processing. Anthropic's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy, available at [https://www.anthropic.com/privacy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy).

### 5.2 Tavily

We use Tavily's search API to retrieve current and relevant legal information, case law, and legal resources. Search queries derived from your legal questions may be sent to Tavily for processing. Tavily's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy.

### 5.3 MongoDB Atlas

We use MongoDB Atlas as our database provider for secure and scalable data storage. MongoDB Atlas maintains comprehensive security certifications and compliance standards. Their privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy, available at [https://www.mongodb.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://www.mongodb.com/legal/privacy-policy).

### 5.4 Data Sharing Principles

When sharing data with third-party services:

- We share only the minimum data necessary for the service to function.
- We do not share personally identifiable information unless required for the specific service.
- All third-party integrations use encrypted connections.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

### 6.1 Retention Period

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. Chat history is retained to allow you to reference previous conversations and to improve our AI system.

### 6.2 Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. Upon account deletion:

- All personal information (name, phone number, location) will be permanently deleted.
- All chat history and conversation data will be permanently deleted.
- All preferences and settings will be permanently deleted.
- Deletion is irreversible and will be completed within 30 days of the request.

To delete your account, use the account deletion option within the App settings or contact us at privacy@legalsahay.com.

### 6.3 Anonymized Data

Anonymized and aggregated data that cannot be used to identify you may be retained indefinitely for analytical and improvement purposes.

## 7. Your Rights

### 7.1 Under Indian Law

In accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

- Access your personal data held by us.
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
- Withdraw consent for data processing.
- Request deletion of your personal data.
- File a grievance regarding data processing practices.

### 7.2 Under GDPR (for EU/EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you additionally have the right to:

- Data portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
- Restriction of processing: request that we limit how we use your data.
- Object to processing: object to our processing of your personal data.
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

## 8. No Sale of Personal Data
