## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Jurryi is your personal AI-powered legal assistant, designed specifically for Indian citizens. Whether you're dealing with a property dispute, criminal matter, family issue, consumer complaint, or any other legal concern — Jurryi provides personalized, jurisdiction-aware legal guidance right from your phone.

🏛️ KEY FEATURES:

• Personalized Legal Guidance — Tell us your state, district, and legal issue, and get advice tailored to YOUR jurisdiction and situation.

• Updated for New Laws — All guidance references the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) — India's new criminal laws effective from July 2024.

• Multilingual Support — Get responses in English, Hindi (हिन्दी), or Hinglish — the way you naturally communicate.

• Actionable Steps — Not just legal theory. Get specific forms to file, offices to visit, documents to carry, fees to pay, and realistic timelines.

• Free Legal Aid Information — Automatically informed about NALSA, DLSA, and other free legal aid resources if you're eligible.

• Safety First — Immediate emergency resources (Women Helpline 181, Police 112) for domestic violence and safety situations.

• Real-time Legal Search — Access the latest case law, amendments, and legal news from trusted Indian legal sources.

📋 COVERS ALL MAJOR LEGAL AREAS:
- Property Disputes & Real Estate
- Criminal Matters (FIR, Bail, Arrest Rights)
- Family Law (Divorce, Custody, Maintenance)
- Consumer Complaints
- Employment & Labour Issues
- Business Contracts
- RTI Applications
- Traffic & Vehicle Issues
- Cyber Crime
- Tenant & Landlord Disputes
- Cheque Bounce Cases (Section 138 NI Act)
- Domestic Violence Protection

⚖️ IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:
Jurryi provides legal information and guidance, not legal advice. It is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified advocate. No attorney-client relationship is created through use of this app.

🔒 PRIVACY:
Your conversations are private and encrypted. We do not sell your data to third parties. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Built with ❤️ for India's 1.4 billion citizens who deserve accessible legal guidance.
