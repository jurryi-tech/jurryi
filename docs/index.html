Jurryi - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Jurryi. Jurryi is an AI-powered legal information assistant designed for users in India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

By using Jurryi, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Phone number (used as primary identifier), full name, and optionally email address.
  • Location Information: State and district (user-selected, not GPS-based) for jurisdiction-specific legal guidance.
  • Profile Information: Age, gender, occupation, income category, legal issue type, language preference, and urgency level.
  • Chat Messages: Messages you send to the AI assistant and the AI-generated responses.
  • Problem Description: Optional description of your legal situation provided during onboarding.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Conversation count, last active timestamp, and app interaction patterns.
  • Device Information: Device type and operating system version (collected by Expo for crash reporting).

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos, videos, or files
  • SMS messages or call logs
  • Financial or payment information
  • Health or biometric data
  • Browsing history

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalized Legal Guidance: Your location and legal issue type are used to provide jurisdiction-specific information.
  • AI Responses: Chat messages are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate relevant legal information.
  • Legal Search: When you ask about recent legal developments, anonymized queries may be sent to our search provider (Tavily) to find current information.
  • Account Management: Phone number is used for authentication. Name is used to personalize the experience.
  • Service Improvement: Aggregated, anonymized usage data helps us improve the app.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Processes chat messages to generate AI responses. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Tavily: Performs legal information searches. Queries are anonymized.
  • Google Cloud (Firestore): Stores user data securely on Google Cloud infrastructure in the Asia-South1 (Mumbai) region.
  • Expo (EAS): Provides app build and update services.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

5. Data Security

  • All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a cost factor of 12 and are never stored in plain text.
  • Authentication uses JWT tokens with short expiry (15 minutes) and secure refresh tokens.
  • Our database is hosted on Google Cloud with enterprise-grade security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen in the app.
  • When you delete your account, ALL associated data is permanently removed, including: your profile information, all conversations, all chat messages, and any other data linked to your account.
  • Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (via the Profile screen)
  • Correct inaccurate data (via the Profile edit feature)
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent by discontinuing use of the app

8. Children's Privacy

Jurryi is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete such information.

9. Legal Compliance

This policy is designed to comply with:

  • The Information Technology Act, 2000 (India)
  • The Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011
  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)
  • Google Play Store Developer Program Policies

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, through in-app notifications.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at:
